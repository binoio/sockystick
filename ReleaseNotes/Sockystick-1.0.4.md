# Sockystick 1.0.4

Sockystick 1.0.4 features an updated app icon: a cozy, highly detailed 3D chunky knitted wool sock with retro stripes on a subtle matte slate gradient, filling the macOS squircle.

### What's New
- **Tactile Knitted Sock App Icon**: Replaced the icon with a beautifully rendered 3D chunky knit sock with realistic wool yarn texture and retro stripes on a subtle matte slate-blue background.
- **Squircle Perfection**: Perfectly framed to fill the macOS squircle with smooth, border-free coverage.
